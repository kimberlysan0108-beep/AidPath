# AidPath — provisional PRD

Status: implementation hypothesis, not a validated research finding.

Problem: fragmented university support pages make it difficult to identify the first appropriate action.
User: a Berkeley student facing a food, housing, unexpected-expense or assistive-technology need.
Current workflow: search, compare pages, interpret conditions, choose an office, prepare information.
Assumed pain points: overlapping programs, urgency, unknown eligibility, and unclear next steps. These are from the brief, not interviews.
Experience: choose a need, answer short relevant questions, inspect reasons and unknowns, save a checklist, open the official source, optionally rate confidence.
Primary success metric, defined before study: median time from scenario start to a correctly identified official resource, adjudicated using a pre-agreed rubric. Source-open time alone is only a proxy.
Secondary metrics: correct-resource rate, completion, source-open rate, confidence, trust and wrong-resource rate.
Non-goals: final eligibility, generic chatbot, document collection, financial/legal advice, broad student dashboard.
Discovery gate: interview 8–10 potential users using docs/DISCOVERY.md before freezing the specification. The implementation is intentionally revisable.
Acceptance: all recommendations have version/source/fingerprint; required failures do not appear; unknowns are clear; changed/stale sources are suppressed; plans survive reload; another session cannot read them; deletion works.
