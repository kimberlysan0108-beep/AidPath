A student legal consultation might include (but is not limited to) one of the following examples: -A landlord-tenant dispute -A citation for a criminal infraction or misdemeanor -Filing an action in California Small Claims Court -Questions related to debt and/or collection actions Berkeley Rent Board For tenants' rights issues (eviction &amp; security deposit, leases, etc.). Call a housing counselor at 510-981-7368 or schedule an appointment with a housing counselor through their website. Bay Area Legal Aid Bay Area Legal Aid helps low-income tenants obtain or remain in safe, affordable housing and provides legal assistance in the areas of eviction defense, housing conditions, fair housing and housing discrimnation East Bay Community Law Center Housing attorneys and advocates can provide assistance on eviction defense, general tenants’ rights issues.
