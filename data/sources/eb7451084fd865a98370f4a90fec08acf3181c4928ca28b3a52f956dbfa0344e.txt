CalFresh is a federally funded program, also known as SNAP/EBT, that provides eligible students with up to $298 to purchase food. CalFresh benefits are loaded onto an EBT card which can be used at most major grocery stores, the Berkeley Student Food Collective, and select farmers markets. Local Programs that Increase Employability Update Beginning June 1, 2026, all associate and bachelor's degree programs at California public colleges and universities (CCC, CSU, and UC) are designated as Local Programs that Increase Employability (LPIE). As a result, most UC Berkeley undergraduate students now meet one of the student eligibility requirements for CalFresh regardless of their major. Students must still meet all other CalFresh eligibility requirements, including income limits and citizenship or lawful permanent resident (LPR) status. Graduate-level students (including Master’s and PhD programs) remain under previous guidance. Graduate students must qualify through an approved program or another eligible student exemption to qualify for CalFresh benefits. Are You Eligible for CalFresh? Check the CalFresh eligibility guidelines and complete a CalFresh prescreener I’m Eligible and Would Like Help Applying The application takes around 45-60 minutes and you should receive your benefits within 30 days. We highly recommend applying with an experienced CalFresh Ambassador. Students are encouraged to create an account on BenefitsCal and collect proper documents prior to their appointments.
