This support is designed to offer immediate relief and help students remain focused on their academic and personal well-being. The Basic Needs Emergency Fund is a collaboration between the Basic Needs Center, Financial Aid &amp; Scholarships Office and Graduate Division. FUND ELIGIBILITY: Enrolled in the current term Exhausted all financial resources Have NOT received funds from the Basic Needs Emergency Fund within the last 2 years Meeting all three criteria does NOT guarantee financial assistance as the Basic Needs Emergency Fund team reviews each submission individually to determine funding eligibility. Our team will share alternate resources with students who are denied funding. What the Fund Covers Rent Financial assistance for up to one month of rent for students experiencing new/unexpected loss of income, safety needs (displacement, SVSH, other safety needs), or students unable to afford rent due to emergency circumstances Food Food assistance for students with short-term food needs that exceed what the Basic Needs Food Pantry can provide. Eligible students must be in the process of applying to CalFresh, have experienced a lapse in or termination of their CalFresh benefits, be ineligible for CalFresh, or have experienced other extenuating circumstances Other Emergency/Safety Needs One-time financial assistance to address immediate needs brought up by an emergency situation with considerations for emergency, safety, or assistive needs Housing Security Deposit Financial assistance for paying a housing security deposit (Requires providing a copy of your lease agreement) Timeline Response times can take up to 7-10 week days. Please consider a Short-Term Emergency Loan for immediate needs. The Basic Needs Emergency Fund team reviews each submission individually to determine funding eligibility, and our staff will share alternate resources with you if you are denied funding.
