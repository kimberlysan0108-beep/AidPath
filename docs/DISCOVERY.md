# Discovery plan — not conducted

Recruit 8–10 consenting adults from first-year, transfer, first-generation and resource-user populations, plus peer advisors. Do not claim completed interviews.

Explain voluntary participation, no impact on aid or grades, optional questions, no collection of account credentials/documents. Ask permission before recording; default to deidentified notes without recording.

1. Tell me about the last time you needed university support.
2. Where did you start? Walk through your actual steps.
3. What was confusing or time-consuming?
4. Which facts made you trust or distrust the page?
5. Did you abandon the process or contact the wrong office?
6. What did you need to do next, and how did you know?
7. What would you prefer not to tell a navigator?
8. Only afterward: try the prototype and describe surprises.

Record participant pseudonym, broad segment, observed friction, exact quote with consent, design implication, and confidence. Separate observations from interpretation. Summarize patterns and counterexamples before revising PRD. Do not convert one anecdote into a universal result.
